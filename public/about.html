About Us

Built Like Good Code

Ctrl+Alt+Brew is where code meets coffee. Inspired by the late-night coding sessions, endless debugging, and deadline rushes of web development students, we serve coffee that fuels creativity and keeps ideas flowing. Every cup is brewed to help you power through your next line of code, fix that stubborn bug, or finish your project before the deadline. Press Ctrl. Press Alt. Brew on.

Why Ctrl+Alt+Brew

We started as web development students who rely on coffee to keep us awake, stay energized, and push through every line of code before the deadline. Between late-night coding sessions, debugging errors, and finishing projects, coffee became our go-to fuel. That’s why we created Ctrl+Alt+Brew—a cafe built for students, developers, and dreamers who need a little extra boost to bring their ideas to life. Whether you're fixing bugs or racing against a deadline, we’ve got the coffee to keep you going.

Built for Builders

A few things on this site that make Ctrl+Alt+Brew different.

Ctrl+Alt Shortcuts

Navigate without the mouse. Press Ctrl+Alt+H for Home, M for Menu, A for About, and C for Contact. The hint bar at the bottom of each page reminds you. Shortcuts are off while you type in a form.

Terminal Ordering

On the Menu page, order like a developer. Type menu to list drinks, order latte to add one, cart to review, and checkout to finish.

Caffeine Meter

Every drink shows a caffeine level from 0 to 100%, so you can pick a gentle sip for the afternoon or a full boost for the all-nighter.

Featured Drinks

Our specialty drinks are highlighted at the top of the Menu page, loaded live from the server so the list always matches what we serve.
